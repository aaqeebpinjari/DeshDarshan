Cataract Surgery in Ancient India

Sushruta, the father of surgery, pioneered cataract surgery in India around 600 BCE, laying the foundation of modern ophthalmology.

📖 Origin & Significance

The Indian physician Sushruta, in his medical text Sushruta Samhita, described cataract surgery techniques as early as 600 BCE. His pioneering work established India as a leader in early surgical practices.

💡 Did You Know?

👁️ Sushruta used a curved needle to remove cataracts.
📚 His text, Sushruta Samhita, is among the oldest surgical manuals.
🌍 Cataract surgery spread from India to Greece and later to the West.
🏥 India is still a global leader in ophthalmology today.

📜 Historical Timeline

  • 600 BCE – Sushruta describes cataract surgery in Sushruta Samhita.
  • 1st century CE – Techniques spread to Greece and Rome.
  • 18th century – Western medicine adopts surgical cataract removal.
  • 21st century – India continues as a global hub for eye care.
