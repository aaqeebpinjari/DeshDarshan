Aadhaar

The world’s largest biometric identity system, empowering citizens with secure, unique digital identification.

📖 Origin & Significance

Launched in 2009 by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI), Aadhaar provides a unique 12-digit identification number to every resident of India. It uses biometric and demographic data to ensure authenticity, enabling secure access to government schemes, financial services, and digital platforms.

💡 Did You Know?

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Aadhaar covers over 1.3 billion people.
📲 It simplifies KYC verification for banking and SIM cards.
💸 Aadhaar enabled direct transfer of subsidies to citizens.
🌍 Largest digital ID program in the world.

📜 Historical Timeline

  • 2009 – Aadhaar project launched by UIDAI.
  • 2010s – Used for government schemes, welfare, and banking KYC.
  • 2020s – Became the foundation of India’s digital identity ecosystem.
