Ayurveda

An ancient Indian system of medicine and holistic healing that emphasizes balance between mind, body, and spirit, still guiding wellness worldwide.

📖 Origin & Significance

Ayurveda, meaning "Science of Life," originated in India over 3,000 years ago. Documented in the Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita, it introduced principles of diet, herbal medicine, detoxification, yoga, and surgery. Its philosophy focuses on balancing the three doshas — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha — for complete well-being.

💡 Did You Know?

🌿 Ayurveda is one of the world’s oldest medical systems still practiced today.
👨‍⚕️ Sushruta is considered the Father of Surgery, describing over 300 procedures.
🍵 Ayurveda prescribes personalized diets based on body type and doshas.
🧘 Yoga and meditation are integral parts of Ayurvedic healing.

📜 Historical Timeline

  • 1500 BCE – Vedic texts mention early concepts of healing and herbal remedies.
  • 600 BCE – Sushruta writes Sushruta Samhita, detailing surgery and anatomy.
  • 200 CE – Charaka compiles Charaka Samhita, systematizing internal medicine.
  • 20th century – Ayurveda regains global popularity as alternative medicine.
