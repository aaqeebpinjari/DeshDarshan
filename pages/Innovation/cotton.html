Cotton & Textile Weaving

India was the birthplace of cotton cultivation and textile weaving, revolutionizing global clothing and trade.

📖 Origin & Significance

India is the earliest known cultivator of cotton, dating back to the Indus Valley Civilization (around 2500 BCE). Indian textiles became world famous, influencing trade routes, cultures, and economies worldwide.

💡 Did You Know?

🧵 Cotton was cultivated in the Indus Valley over 4,000 years ago.
🌍 Indian textiles were exported to Egypt, Rome, and China.
👗 Muslin and calico fabrics from India were world-renowned.
💰 The textile trade made India a major hub in global commerce.

📜 Historical Timeline

  • 2500 BCE – Indus Valley Civilization cultivates cotton.
  • 1st century CE – Indian textiles traded across Asia and Europe.
  • 17th century – India dominates global cotton exports.
  • 19th century – British industrial revolution impacts Indian textile trade.
